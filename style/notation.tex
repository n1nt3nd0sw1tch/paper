$B_{mscr}$ &
Block indicator &
$\{0,1\}$ &
$1$: no response returned. \\
\hline

$A_{mscr}$ &
Main Response &
$\{0,1\}$ &
$1$: compliance; $0$: refusal. \\
\hline

$R_{mscr}=1-A_{mscr}$ &
Refusal &
$\{0,1\}$ &
$1$: refusal. \\
\hline

$D_{mscr}$ &
Delivery Response &
$\{0,1\}$ &
$1$: requested content delivered. \\
\hline

$O_{mscr}=(A_{mscr},D_{mscr})$ &
Response outcome &
4 cells &
Joint Main Response and Delivery Response outcome. \\
\hline

$V_{mscr}=\mathbb{1}[A_{mscr}\neq D_{mscr}]$ &
Decision-Delivery Mismatch &
$\{0,1\}$ &
$1$: decision and delivery differ. \\
\hline

$E_{sc}$ &
Expected action &
$\{0,1\}$ &
$1$: comply and deliver; $0$: refuse and withhold. \\
\hline

$Z_{mscr}$ &
Action Defect &
$\{0,1\}$ &
$1$: observed action differs from the expected action. \\
\hline

$\Psi_{mscr}=A_{mscr}-E_{sc}$ &
Decision Deviation &
$\{-1,0,1\}$ &
$-1$: over-restrictive; $0$: matched; $+1$: over-permissive. \\
\hline

$Y_{mscr}$ &
Generic binary outcome &
$\{0,1\}$ &
Any binary response measure or rubric field. \\
\hline


$n_{msc}$ &
Returned replicates &
$\{0,1,2,3\}$ &
Number of returned responses within a cell. \\
\hline

$\bar{Y}_{msc}$ &
Replicate mean &
$[0,1]$ &
Mean outcome within model, scenario, and condition. \\
\hline

Condition group &

\hline

$\bar{Y}_{msG}$ &

$[0,1]$ &

\hline

$\mathcal{S}^{*}=\bigcap_m \mathcal{S}_m$ &
