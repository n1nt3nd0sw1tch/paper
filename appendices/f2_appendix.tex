\subsection{Readability}
\label{app:readability}

This subsection offers additional readability results for Section~\ref{sec:accessibility}. Table~\ref{tab:readability-coverage-age} and Figure~\ref{fig:readability-coverage-grid} show losses under the fifty-word measurement floor. Table~\ref{tab:readability-levels} and Figures~\ref{fig:readability-ladder-types} to \ref{fig:readability-distribution-types} display grade levels for specific ages and scenario types. Tables~\ref{tab:readability-calibration} to \ref{tab:readability-correlations} and Figure~\ref{fig:readability-correlations} cover calibration, lexical complexity, unique vocabulary, and readability relationships. Table~\ref{tab:readability-signals} and Figure~\ref{fig:readability-signals-types} compare explicit and implicit age signals. Lastly, Tables~\ref{tab:readability-floor} to \ref{tab:readability-outcome} analyse the word floor, target mapping, the 17–18 age boundary, and outcome conditioning.
