\paragraph{Block Rate}

Of 46,800 requests, 160 were blocked: 159 by Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite and one by
Claude Haiku 4.5, leaving 46,640 returned replies. Table~\ref{tab:corpus-blocking}
reports blocking by age and affected domain. Table~\ref{tab:safety-blocking} reports the paired blocking contrasts. Gemini
retained 20 of 25 Age Restricted scenarios for these comparisons; all other
models retained all 25. Including blocked requests in the denominator changed
Macro-Average directional safety rates by less than one percentage point.

\paragraph{Rater Agreement}
\label{app:collection}
\label{app:classification}

Responses were generated under common decoding settings and classified using
the thirteen-label rubric in Section~\ref{subsec:properties}.
Table~\ref{tab:classification} reports classification runtime. Agreement was evaluated on 600 manually annotated replies. The classifier
received the canonical request and response, but not the age condition, scenario
type or expected response. Reasoning increased agreement for \texttt{Answer}
from $\kappa=0.919$ to $0.978$, while median agreement remained similar overall;
reasoning was therefore retained for the final classification.
Tables~\ref{tab:reasoning-fields} and~\ref{tab:judge-model-agreement} report
agreement by label and model. Table~\ref{tab:second-judge} compares the human annotation (H), classifier of
record (C1), and second LLM judge (C2). Values below the $\kappa=0.70$ threshold
are shaded; Companion Identity was too rare for meaningful agreement estimates.
