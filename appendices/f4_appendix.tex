\section{Experiment 2}
\label{app:dialogue}

This section provides additional results for Experiment 2.
Tables~\ref{tab:dialogue-yield}--\ref{tab:dialogue-leakage} report collection
coverage and delivery among refusals.
Tables~\ref{tab:dialogue-retention}--\ref{tab:dialogue-contrasts} report
retention, outcome trajectories, directional failures and differences between
attack methods.
Tables~\ref{tab:dialogue-ladder}--\ref{tab:dialogue-memory-gap} report age
conditioning, the 17--18 boundary and adjusted safety-memory estimates.
Table~\ref{tab:dialogue-domains} reports Action Defect by harm domain.
