Section~\ref{sec:semantics}. Table~\ref{tab:semantic-coverage} reports embedding
coverage, while Table~\ref{tab:semantic-complete} compares available- and
complete-case estimates under MiniLM and MPNet.
Table~\ref{tab:semantic-marginals} reports separation after adjustment for
response-length differences, and Table~\ref{tab:semantic-diagnostics} reports
correlations with response length, lexical distance and outcome changes.
