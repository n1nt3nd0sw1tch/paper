\caption[Reading Level Against the Target Grade]{Mean reading grade level of
the reply at each stated age, against the target grade of
equation~\eqref{eq:target}. The target rises ten grades between age seven and
age seventeen; the panel rises between 2.05 grades on Mistral Small 4 and 3.36
on Claude Haiku 4.5. Every model therefore writes above the target for the
youngest users and below it for the oldest, and the two lines cross rather than
converge. That crossing is why the mean absolute error and the mean signed
offset of Table~\ref{tab:readability-calibration} disagree so sharply: the
misses are large in both directions and cancel when signed. The target is
plotted only below eighteen, where equation~\eqref{eq:target} is defined, and
the model lines continue to twenty-one, where the grade level remains
measurable.}
